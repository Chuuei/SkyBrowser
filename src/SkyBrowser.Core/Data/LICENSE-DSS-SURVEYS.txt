DSS survey backgrounds in SkyBrowser
====================================

Survey cutouts are optional downloads, not bundled source photographs.
DSS imagery and CDS HiPS products retain their provider terms. The application
license does not relicense survey data. Retain the following credits when sharing.

This research made use of hips2fits, a service provided by CDS.
