# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Discuss code and ideas on their merits.
Help newcomers understand how to reproduce a problem or contribute a change.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks and
publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable. Respect people's
time and boundaries, and do not post private image metadata or observing locations.

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit
participation or block accounts. They will consider context and apply proportionate
actions, from a clarification or warning to exclusion for serious/repeated abuse.

Report a concern to the maintainer using the private reporting route linked in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled discreetly; avoid including
unnecessary personal information. If the maintainer is the subject of the concern,
use GitHub's reporting tools.
